Privacy Policy

Effective 12 November 2026

Who we are

Row One is published by MILIVON ("we"), a sole proprietorship registered in Singapore. If you have any questions about this policy or your data, write to support@milivon.com.

The short version

Row One has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Your flights, notes and goals stay on your iPhone. We don't run servers for the app and don't receive or store your logbook.

What's stored on your iPhone

Everything you add to Row One is stored in the app on your device: flights (airports, dates, times, flight numbers, seats, cabin class, booking references, aircraft and notes), your goals and your settings. If you connect TripIt, your private feed link is kept in the iPhone's Keychain.

This data is part of your normal device backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup, depending on your settings). You can delete individual flights at any time; deleting the app removes all of its data from the device.

When Row One connects to the internet

The app works offline. It only goes online for the features below, and connects directly from your iPhone to the service named; nothing passes through us:

  • Aircraft photos: an aircraft's registration (for example D-AIXP) is sent to Planespotters.net to find a photo.
  • Destination weather: the arrival airport's location and the date are sent to Apple's WeatherKit.
  • TripIt import, only if you add your feed link: the link is used to download your trips from TripIt.
  • Maps are provided by Apple Maps.
  • AeroDataBox flight search, only if you tap "Find the tail on AeroDataBox": the AeroDataBox website opens inside the app, and the flight number is copied to your clipboard so you can paste it there. Row One sends AeroDataBox nothing itself; what you search on its website is covered by AeroDataBox's own privacy policy.

Like any internet request, these services see your device's IP address. They handle it under their own privacy policies. We don't send them your name, your logbook or anything that identifies you.

Permissions

Camera: used to scan boarding pass barcodes and aircraft tail numbers. The image is processed on your iPhone and isn't stored or sent anywhere.

Photos: when you pick a boarding pass photo or screenshot, only that image is read, on your iPhone. When you save a poster of your flights, iOS asks once whether Row One may add images to your library; that permission only adds images and doesn't let the app see your photos.

Calendar: Row One asks for full calendar access only if you use Import from Calendar. Your calendar is read on your iPhone to find events with a flight number; nothing is uploaded. Add to Calendar uses Apple's own screen and needs no access.

You can change these permissions at any time in the iPhone's Settings app.

Children

Row One isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Your rights

Because MILIVON doesn't receive personal data through the app, we usually hold no information about you, apart from any e-mails you send us. You stay in control of your data on your device. Wherever you live, you can contact us at support@milivon.com with questions or requests about your data.

If you contact us

If you e-mail us, we use your address and message only to reply to you, and delete them when they're no longer needed.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and in the app with a new effective date. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's update notes.
