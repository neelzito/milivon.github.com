Tyniy

Mac, iPhone & iPad · Free

Much smaller photos.
No visible difference.

Tyniy shrinks photos and videos while keeping them visually identical to the originals, so you get your storage back without giving up quality.

  • Encoded with jpegli, tuned to what the human eye can actually see
  • Keeps EXIF, GPS, dates, albums and colour profiles
  • Originals stay restorable from the Trash or Recently Deleted
  • 100% on-device: no ads, no subscriptions, no uploads

Principles

How we build.

Four rules behind every MILIVON product, from the first sketch to the App Store and every update after.

01

Private by default

Your photos, flights and data stay on your device. No accounts to create, and your data is never sold or shared.

02

Focused

One clear job per app, done properly. Fewer settings to learn, more time back for you.

03

Native & meticulous

Built with Apple's own frameworks, so the apps feel at home on your device and sweat the small details.

04

Made to last

Honest design and steady updates. Software you can rely on for years, not weeks.

The studio

Small studio. High standards.

MILIVON is an independent software studio based in Singapore. We design, engineer, ship and support our own products end to end, which means every detail, from the first pixel to the reply in your inbox, gets the same care.

Our business is simple: we make software people choose to use. No advertising, and no selling of data.
